Ton Pote le Geek ("your geek friend") is my own freelance business. I build automated systems and custom tools for entrepreneurs and small businesses: connecting the tools they already use — CRM, email, payments, appointment scheduling — to remove hours of manual work every week, and applications for their own clients. For those who want to own their automation system rather than rent it, I deploy self-hosted n8n infrastructure. The most complete engagement is a profitability calculator for a training business: it started as an iframe with no database and became a full application once usage showed that users came back — about 400 accounts created, 300 active —, and I am now turning it into a product sold under my own name. Same standards as my enterprise engagements: versioned, tested, documented, built to run without me.
