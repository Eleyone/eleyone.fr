Designed and implemented on-chain financial flows for the Chiliz ecosystem (Socios.com, fan tokens), within a domain-driven microservices architecture — mainly on the wallet service, plus the NFT and user services.

- On-chain batch processing architecture: multiple operations across different wallets grouped into a single chained workflow, improving traceability, reconciliation and failure handling.
- Service decompositions, to narrow service scopes and keep domain boundaries clean.
- Migration of Chiliz-issued tokens from an Ethereum fork to Chiliz Chain.
- Migration of custodial wallets to non-custodial, integrating the DFNS provider.
- Internal trading application designed and built from scratch (Symfony, Symfony UX): deposits, withdrawals and swaps across multiple DEXs, then extended multi-chain.
- Internal indexer (TypeScript, viem.js): collecting on-chain transactions for verification and reconciliation, and computing trading decision-support KPIs.
