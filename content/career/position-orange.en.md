Orange is France's main telecom operator. On its CRV application — the visit reports of field sales reps selling telecom equipment to businesses —, a legacy Zend Framework 1 application, read-only on an Oracle database managed elsewhere in the IT system, I refactored 90% of the code for performance and maintainability, with no production downtime. I measured where the time went before touching the code, and settled on a reversible trade-off: pages that took seconds came down to milliseconds. With no tech lead on the team, I was the one making the technical decisions. I also designed and built a new Zend Framework 2 application with a BackboneJS and Marionette frontend. The assignment was carried out through Modis, an IT consultancy.
